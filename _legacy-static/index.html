01About Everis

About Everis

Everis is a developer and investor in African energy infrastructure. We take projects from the first identification of an opportunity to the long-term ownership of an operating asset, bringing technical, commercial and financial discipline to each stage.

Depending on the opportunity, Everis may act as project sponsor, developer, investor, acquirer or shareholder. Our involvement can cover the full project lifecycle, usually through dedicated project companies.

03Investment Philosophy

Selective by design. Built to endure.

Energy assets run for decades. We commit capital only where a project can stand up technically, commercially and financially for that long.

  1. I

    Commercial discipline

    Each opportunity must answer a clear energy need, have a creditworthy route to market and sound project economics before we commit to it.

  2. II

    Risk management

    We assess technical, contractual, financial and counterparty risks early, and allocate them in the project structure to the parties best placed to manage them.

  3. III

    Practical execution

    We put coordinated delivery ahead of theoretical plans, overseeing engineering, procurement and construction with the operating phase in mind.

  4. IV

    Long-term value creation

    We invest as owners, managing assets through operation to keep them reliable, well governed and valuable over the long term.

04Our Approach

From opportunity to operating asset.

One sequence, from first screening to long-term ownership.

  1. 01

    Identify

    Source and screen opportunities where the energy need is clear and the fundamentals look sound, whether greenfield, brownfield or operating.

  2. 02

    Evaluate

    Assess technical feasibility, resource and site conditions, offtake, project economics and key risks.

  3. 03

    Structure

    Establish the contractual, ownership and investment structures, from PPAs and concessions to joint ventures and co-investments.

  4. 04

    Finance

    Arrange equity and debt with lenders, investors and project partners on terms that suit the asset’s life.

  5. 05

    Deliver & Manage

    Coordinate delivery, oversee construction and manage the asset through operation as a long-term owner.
